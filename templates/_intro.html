ReconX

Reconnaissance Information Collector

ReconX gathers publicly available technical information about a domain and turns it into one clear dashboard and a PDF report. It is passive by design: it only reads what a website already shows to everyone.

For educational use and authorized security assessments only.

1

Discover

IP addresses, DNS records, registration details and subdomains.

2

Identify

Technologies a site appears to use, marked as reported or suspected.

3

Review

HTTPS and security headers, each with a plain recommendation.

4

Report

Every scan is saved to history and can be downloaded as a PDF.
